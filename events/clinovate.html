ABG Analysis

About

This hands-on workshop will provide comprehensive training in ABG sampling, interpretation of acid–base disorders, and rapid bedside decision-making—skills that are indispensable in emergency and critical care settings. Mastering ABG analysis enables clinicians to identify respiratory and metabolic disturbances early and initiate timely, life-saving interventions.
